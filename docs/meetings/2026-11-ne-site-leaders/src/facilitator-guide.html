NE Site Leader working session · 60 minutes

From Flags to Ownership

The one idea: attendance is holding (90.1%, goal 80%). What’s slipping is reliability around the session: coverage, cancellations, and closing the loop in Pearl. A flag is a conversation that happened too late. We want the region to hear it from the site first.

NextSL tells the classroom teacher or counselor per the school’s process, and tells the PM the same day. The tutor checks in gently next session.

If you hear pushback

“Tutors don’t have time to close Pearl before they leave.”It takes about two minutes a session, and it happens during paid time. The SL sweep catches anything missed while the tutor is still in the building.
“Teachers won’t release scholars. That’s not on us.”Agreed, so it isn’t yours to fix alone. It’s yours to raise. The first conversation with the teacher is the SL’s. If it repeats, the PM takes it to administration.
“I can’t cover when tutors are out.”That’s exactly when to escalate, before the session. Cancel with the right reason so the data tells the truth about coverage.
“The flags are wrong.”Sometimes they are. Send it with the four-line message: what happened, what you checked. Flags that look wrong get fixed faster when you send them up.
“This is more work on top of everything.”These routines replace the chase emails. A five-minute sweep costs less than three days of back-and-forth.

How to read it
  • Scholar attendance follows the partner-report definition (tutor absences and school closures don’t count against scholars). Lyndhurst MS only began in late October, so its numbers are small.
  • Misses: tutor absent counts scholar seats lost because the tutor was out. This is the region’s #1 miss reason (189 in two weeks). Clifton ES and Hudson MS carry most of it.
  • Uncovered sessions were cancelled as “Tutor Vacancy.” Silk City and Clifton MS show an open position, not a daily routine problem. Confirm the hiring status before the meeting.
  • Left Incomplete / not recorded are pure routine flags, and the end-of-day sweep removes them. Clifton MS, Hudson MS, and Paterson ES are the places to start.
  • Cancelled, no reason can’t be fixed after the fact (Pearl locks it), only prevented. Clifton ES and Paterson MS account for most of them.
