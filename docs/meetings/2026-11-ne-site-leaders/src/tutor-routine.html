Before, during, after

Your scholars count on you showing up and your data showing what happened. Three habits keep both on track.

1

Before

10 minutes ahead
  • Open Pearl: check today’s schedule and roster.
  • Lesson plan ready for every group.
  • Room confirmed. If not, tell your Site Leader now.
  • Running late or out? Text your Site Leader before the session, not after.
2

During

In the session
  • Start on time. Collect scholars if needed.
  • Mark attendance as scholars arrive.
  • Note anything that blocks learning: behavior, room, roster.
3

After

Before you leave the building
  • Close every session: attendance, missed reason, and survey.
  • Never leave a missed reason blank.
  • Comment for admin if anything needs follow-up.
  • Tell your Site Leader about any roadblock today.
Nobody leaves the building with an open session.

Which missed reason do I pick?

Whole group can’t meet (assembly, event, schedule change, half day)Tell your Site Leader right away. They cancel it in Pearl with the reason.

Learn the real reason later? Update it in Pearl. Surveys can be added for 7 days after the session.

Tell your Site Leader

Right away: you’re late or out · the whole group is gone · no room · any worry about a scholar’s safety or wellbeing

Same day: behavior that stops learning · roster or Pearl problems · something a scholar said that worries you

Always to your Site Leader, not to school administration. They’ll get it to the right person.
