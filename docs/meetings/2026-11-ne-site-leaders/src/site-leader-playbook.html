Site Leader playbook

A week where the region never has to ask

You own the site: coverage, teacher relationships, and closing the loop in Pearl. These routines keep flags from reaching the region and keep scholars in their sessions.

Daily
Morning · before first block
Coverage check: who’s out, who covers, which sessions to regroup or cancel with a reason.
Midday
Walk into at least one session. Check that lesson plans match what’s happening.
End of day · 5 minutes
Pearl sweep for today: no session left Scheduled/Incomplete, no blank missed reasons.
Weekly
Monday
Open Site Leader Flags and the Support Report for your school.
Wednesday
Flags cleared, or escalated with the four-line message.
Thursday
Lesson plans checked for next week.
Friday
Teacher touchpoint on pull-outs and schedule changes.
Every two weeks
PM check-in · you bring the snapshot
What’s working · your top two flags · what you already did · what you need from your PM.

What’s on your flags list, and how to stop it

FlagPrevent it by
Session left IncompleteTutors close every session before leaving; your end-of-day sweep.
Missed reason left blank“Scholar Unavailable” when unsure. Never blank.
Cancelled with no reasonPick the reason while cancelling. Pearl locks it afterward.
Whole group logged as missedTutor tells you right away; you cancel in Pearl before the session.
Repeat unavailable scholar2+ in two weeks: ask the teacher at a break or check school attendance.
Uncovered sessionMorning coverage check; tutors report absences before the session.
Scholar kept in classTalk with the teacher this week. If it continues, escalate to your PM.
The end-of-day Pearl sweep
  1. Filter Pearl to today and your school.
  2. Anything still Scheduled? Find the tutor before they leave.
  3. Any scholar Missed with no reason? Fix it now while they remember.
  4. Whole group out? Make sure it was cancelled with a reason, not logged as missed.
  5. Any tutor comment marked for admin? Read it today.
Escalate to your PM when
  • Anyone is unsafe: same day, after the school’s process
  • You can’t cover a session: before it starts
  • It happened twice and your fix didn’t hold
  • It needs a principal’s decision: schedule, room, or a teacher pattern
