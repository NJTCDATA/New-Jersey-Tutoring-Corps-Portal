Escalation protocol

Who to tell, and when

Solve it at your level. Raise it one step when you can’t. Bring what you already tried, and expect an answer back within 24 hours.

1

Is anyone unsafe?

Now

Tell your Site Leader; follow the school’s process. PM hears it same day.

2

Will a session be missed?

Before it starts

Tutor tells the Site Leader, who covers, regroups, or cancels in Pearl with a reason.

3

Has it happened twice?

Up one step

Once is a moment. Twice is a pattern. Raise it with the four-line message.

NowSame day / 24 hrsThis week
Tutor→ Site Leader
  • Running late or out
  • Whole group can’t meet (assembly, trip, testing)
  • No room or space
  • Any worry about a scholar’s safety or wellbeing
  • Behavior that stops learning
  • Roster or Pearl schedule is wrong
  • A scholar comment that worries you
  • Same scholar unavailable or kept in class 2+ times
  • Materials or training you need
Site Leader→ Program Manager
  • Safety concern (after the school’s process)
  • A session you can’t cover
  • A principal asks to change a schedule or room
  • Same issue twice with no fix
  • Tutor professionalism concern
  • Pearl changes you can’t make (roster, schedule)
  • Tutor pattern after coaching: lesson plans, open sessions, lateness
  • Teacher pull-outs that continue after your conversation
Program Manager→ Partner administration
  • Safety, alongside the school’s own process
  • Schedule, room, or roster changes the school asked for
  • Patterns that cost scholars sessions: pull-outs, schedule changes, space
  • Partner expectations and agreements
One NJTC voice
  • Tutors bring concerns to their Site Leader, never directly to school administration.
  • Site Leaders own teacher-level conversations. Anything that needs a principal’s decision goes through your PM.
  • Don’t agree to changes on the spot. Say “Let me loop in my PM so we get it right in Pearl.”
  • Safety comes first. Follow the school’s safeguarding process right away, then tell your PM the same day.
The four-line escalation
What happened
What I already did
What I need
By when

Works by text, email, or in the hallway. If you can’t fill in line 2, try something first (unless it’s safety).
